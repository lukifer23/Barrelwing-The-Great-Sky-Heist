# Playing Barrelwing

The goal of each campaign stage is to reach its exit. Collecting gems, taking high routes, defeating enemies, and finishing quickly improve the score. The last campaign stage requires defeating the Storm Baron before escaping to the exit.

## Movement

The joystick is analog: small horizontal movements walk, larger movements run. The stick also climbs ladders when pushed up or down. A small central dead zone prevents accidental movement. Keyboard movement uses full running speed.

Hold JUMP for a higher ground jump. Release and tap JUMP again while airborne to spend one of the two air flaps. Landing refills flaps. Wind channels switch to continuous forward flight with unlimited rhythmic flaps. DASH bursts horizontally, breaks wooden barrels, and damages enemies, including armored guards. Metal barrels must be avoided.

The touch buttons show remaining air charges and dash recharge. FLAP replaces JUMP in the air, and flight hides DASH because wind channels use flaps only. The field guide has separate touch, keyboard, and powerup tabs.

Down drops through one-way platforms. Springs launch higher than a normal jump. Conveyors move the ground beneath the courier; moving platforms carry them.

## Couriers

Choose a courier from the title or map. The selection applies to a new stage; Continue retains the courier used by that saved run.

| Courier | Trait | Personality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pip | Balanced speed and health | A professional courier with an unprofessional tail |
| Fenn | 10% faster running | Express service, questionable brakes |
| Mallow | One extra heart, 8% slower running | Delivers eventually, survives enthusiastically |

All couriers have the same base jump, two air flaps, and access to permanent upgrades. Sky Sprint uses three hearts for everyone and the same flight speed.

## Pickups

| Pickup | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Acorn | Score; consecutive pickups build a combo; every 100 acorns grants a life |
| Sky Gem | 500 points; three distinct gems per stage |
| Shield | Blocks one damaging hit |
| Speed Boost | 35% faster running for 12 seconds before upgrades |
| Magnet | Pulls nearby acorns toward the courier for 12 seconds before upgrades |
| Extra Flap | Three air flaps instead of two for 15 seconds before upgrades |
| Heart | Restores one missing heart |
| Extra Life | Adds one life and restores health |
| Acorn Nova | Immediately blasts enemies and wooden barrels within 150 world units |
| Wind Crystal | Refills air flap charges |

Timed powers appear in the HUD with names and remaining seconds. Shield remains until it blocks a hit. Acorn Nova is an immediate burst, not an ability button. It does not bypass the boss's vulnerability rules or destroy armored barrels.

## Permanent upgrades

Clear stages to bank newly found gems and improved rating stars. Each unique gem and each rating star earns one Sky Shard. The Windtail Workshop spends these on More Hearts, More Lives, Faster Dash, and Longer Powers. Each has two tiers, costing 3 and 6 shards. The workshop shows the current and next numerical benefit before purchase.

Replaying a stage without improving its saved gems or rating does not create more shards. Temporary pickups and permanent upgrades are separate systems.

## Checkpoints and lives

Flags heal the courier and save a checkpoint. Losing all hearts or falling spends a life and returns to the checkpoint. A manual checkpoint restart also spends a life. At game over, Continue grants another set of starting lives and resets the attempt score and timer. Saved completed-stage records and permanent upgrades remain.

Closing the app saves run progress, but restoration starts at the latest checkpoint rather than at the exact airborne position. Temporary powers and transient hazards reset. See [SAVES.md](SAVES.md) for the stored fields.

## Enemies and fairness

Slugs patrol. Ants warn before rushing. Hoppers wind up and leap. Armored guards need repeated dashes or a Nova. Wisps launch projectiles. Birds pursue, while divers warn before a downward charge. Copper crawlers move in bursts. Storm bees fly faster and flash before firing. Plants guard their position with snapping movement.

The Baron announces attacks and becomes vulnerable between attack sequences. Stomp or dash during those windows. A regular enemy can be stomped only when its anatomy allows it; a snapping plant or armored guard is not a safe landing pad.

Humor is part of the courier world, but warning colors, pickup names, timers, and control labels communicate the actual mechanics.

## Pausing

PAUSE or Escape/P suspends the action in the campaign and Sky Sprint. Android Back and leaving the foreground also pause active gameplay. Settings can be opened without spending powerup duration, dash recharge, protection time, or enemy pattern time. Resume returns to the same session. Sky Sprint banks its current personal best when paused; closing the app ends that Sprint attempt rather than restoring its exact flight position.
