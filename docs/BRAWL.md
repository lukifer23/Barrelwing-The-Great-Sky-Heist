# Barrel Brawl

Barrel Brawl is an original solo-versus-CPU platform-fighting mode. It uses Barrelwing's couriers, art, music, and courier-world humor. It is a separate activity from the campaign and does not replace campaign progression.

## Rules

Each fighter starts with three stocks. Attacks increase damage percentage; higher percentages produce stronger knockback. Leaving the arena's outer bounds loses a stock. The last fighter with stocks remaining wins.

Matches have a three-minute limit. If time expires, the fighter with more stocks wins; equal stocks are resolved by lower damage. An exact tie enters Sudden Delivery: one stock each and amplified knockback.

Respawning resets damage and grants brief invulnerability. The translucency indicates that protection. A completed match records wins, losses, knockouts, and the best winning streak.

## Controls

| Touch | Keyboard | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Joystick | A / D or Left / Right | Move |
| JUMP | Space | Ground jump and two air recovery jumps |
| DASH | J | Short directional burst |
| Hold HIT | Hold K | Repeat attacks as they recharge |
| Joystick up + HIT | Up + K | Upward launch |
| Joystick down + HIT | Down + K | Downward strike |
| PAUSE | Escape / P | Pause, settings, restart, or save and leave |

Release and press JUMP again for each air recovery jump. The campaign's touch controls gain the HIT button only in Brawl. Keyboard and touch inputs use the same fighter logic.

## Fighters and arenas

Pip is balanced. Fenn moves faster and attacks more frequently, but is lighter and easier to launch. Mallow moves more slowly and attacks less frequently, with stronger hits and greater weight. Campaign health upgrades do not affect match stocks.

Canopy Court is the forest arena. Unsafe Workplace has changing conveyor drift. Return to Thunder has periodic air currents. All arenas receive announced barrel deliveries; the warning gives time to react before a barrel enters.

CPU difficulty changes reaction time, attack intervals, recovery decisions, and dash use. CPU attacks have a 200 ms preparation ring before their active hit window. Player attacks prepare in 65 ms. Both use the same character damage, cooldowns, jump charges, and ring-out rules. Damage interrupts a pending attack. Short jump, dash, and attack requests are buffered during recovery; touch buttons show remaining air jumps and action recharge.

Pausing suspends the match clock, initial countdown, protection, and cooldowns. Opening settings or backgrounding the app does not consume that time.

## Saving

The setup menu can resume a saved match. Stocks, damage, courier choices, arena, difficulty, and elapsed time persist. Fighters return to safe starting positions when a saved match resumes. Match records and active Brawl saves are separate from the campaign run. Starting a new campaign keeps Brawl records.

The first version is solo versus CPU. Local controller multiplayer and online play are not implemented.
